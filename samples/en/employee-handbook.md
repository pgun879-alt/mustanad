# Employee Handbook — Nour Technical Services

*This is fictional sample content created to demonstrate `mustanad`. It does not describe a
real company and is not legal or HR advice.*

## 1. Working hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, worked Sunday to Thursday from 08:30 to 16:30 with a
one-hour unpaid break. Remote work is permitted up to two days per week with the written
approval of a direct manager. Requests for a compressed four-day week are reviewed
individually and must be renewed every six months.

## 2. Annual leave

Every full-time employee accrues 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accruing at 1.83 days per completed month of service. Employees in their first year may take
leave only after completing three months of service.

A maximum of 10 unused annual leave days may be carried into the following year. Carried days
expire on 31 March and are not paid out on expiry. Leave requests of five consecutive days or
more must be submitted at least 14 calendar days in advance.

## 3. Sick leave

Paid sick leave is 15 working days per year. A medical certificate is required for any absence
of three consecutive days or longer. Sick leave does not accrue and cannot be carried over.

## 4. Expense reimbursement

Employees are reimbursed for travel, accommodation and client-meeting costs incurred on
company business. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, with an
itemised receipt attached. Claims submitted after 60 days are rejected.

The per-diem allowance for domestic travel is 4,500 DZD per night. International per-diem is
set per destination by the finance team before departure. Taxi fares under 1,200 DZD may be
claimed without a receipt, up to four times per month.

Alcohol, personal entertainment and traffic fines are never reimbursable.

## 5. Equipment

Each engineer receives a laptop on their first day. The device remains company property and
must be returned on the last day of employment. Loss or damage caused by negligence may be
recovered from the employee up to a maximum of 30% of the device's purchase price.

Personal use of company equipment is permitted provided it does not involve storing personal
media libraries, running cryptocurrency mining software, or installing unlicensed software.

## 6. Probation and notice

The probation period is three months, extendable once by a further three months. During
probation either party may terminate with seven days' notice. After probation, the notice
period is 30 days for engineers and 60 days for team leads and above.

## 7. Confidentiality

Client source code, credentials, and commercial terms are confidential indefinitely, including
after employment ends. Client data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud
accounts. Suspected data exposure must be reported to the security contact within 24 hours of
discovery.
