# Support and Service Level Agreement — Nour Technical Services

*Fictional sample content for demonstrating `mustanad`.*

## 1. Support channels

Support is available by email and through the customer portal. Telephone support is included
only in the Business and Enterprise plans. Support hours are 09:00 to 17:00 Sunday to Thursday,
excluding public holidays.

## 2. Severity definitions

**Severity 1 — Critical.** The production service is completely unavailable, or customer data
is at risk. No workaround exists.

**Severity 2 — High.** A major feature is unavailable or incorrect for most users. A partial
workaround may exist.

**Severity 3 — Normal.** A feature is degraded or inconvenient for some users, and a
workaround exists.

**Severity 4 — Low.** Cosmetic issues, documentation errors, and feature requests.

## 3. Response and resolution targets

| Severity | First response | Target resolution | Plans covered |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 hour | 8 working hours | All plans |
| 2 | 4 working hours | 3 working days | All plans |
| 3 | 1 working day | 10 working days | All plans |
| 4 | 3 working days | No committed target | Business, Enterprise |

Response targets are measured from the moment a ticket is created in the portal, not from the
moment an email is sent. Enterprise customers have a 30-minute first-response target for
Severity 1 issues and out-of-hours escalation by telephone.

## 4. Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.5%, the customer may request a service credit of 10% of that
month's fee. If it falls below 99.0%, the credit is 25%. Credits must be requested in writing
within 30 days of the end of the affected month, are applied to future invoices, and are never
paid in cash. The total credit in any month cannot exceed 50% of the monthly fee.

Uptime excludes scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance, and excludes
outages caused by the customer's own configuration changes.

## 5. Exclusions

The SLA does not cover: problems reproduced only in unsupported browsers; third-party
integrations outside our control; issues caused by the customer exceeding documented API rate
limits; and any environment other than production.

## 6. Escalation

If a response target is missed, the customer may escalate to the support lead by replying to
the ticket with the word ESCALATE in the subject line. Escalation does not change the
resolution target but does trigger a written status update every four working hours.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Ticket contents and attachments are retained for 24 months after a ticket is closed, then
permanently deleted. On contract termination, customer production data is deleted within
30 days of the final invoice being settled, and a written deletion confirmation is issued.
Backups are purged on a rolling 35-day cycle.
